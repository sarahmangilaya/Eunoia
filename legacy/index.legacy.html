About Eunoia

Animation and visual arts studio working across 2D animation, illustration, and 3D modeling.

Eunoia Studio

Animation & Visual Direction

Eunoia combines the tactile discipline of physical painting with modern digital animation pipelines. The studio focuses on weight, fluid timing, and clear silhouette design across independent and commercial productions.

"Motion communicates before color or detail does. Understanding gravity, inertia, and line tension is what gives any drawing its life."

Background & Milestones

2018

Foundations in Drawing & Pigments

Began with sketchbooks filled with sumi ink gestures, human anatomy studies, and watercolor washes. Developed an understanding of gravity, line confidence, and the tactile friction of brush on paper.

2020

2D Keyframe Animation & Timing Arcs

Adopted digital display tablets and industry animation software. Mastered core principles of animation: squash and stretch, timing, anticipation, and secondary action across dynamic combat sequences.

2022

Applied Print & Physical Goods

Produced limited physical goods including dye-sublimated desk mats, woven jacquard lanyards, and hot-stamped stationery for international art conventions.

2024

3D Character Modeling & Quad Topology

Expanded into 3D sculpting with ZBrush and Blender. Bridged 2D character silhouette design with production-ready quad topology for real-time and cinematic engines.

PRESENT

Independent Studio Work & Direction

Available for freelance 2D animation, keyframe direction, character design, and 3D modeling for film productions, game studios, and creative teams.
